Consulting FAQ

Starting out? Start here

Should I form an LLC, a corporation, a partnership or a sole proprietorship?

It depends on your liability exposure, how many owners are involved, how you want the profits taxed, and where you plan to take the business. There is no universally right answer, which is exactly why the conversation is free — call and we'll work through your specifics.

What registrations do I need to start a business in New York?

Depending on the business, that can include registering for sales tax, obtaining employment identification numbers, applying for your EIN, and registering with the New York State Department of Labor for payroll tax processing. I'll help you figure out which ones apply and get them filed.

Can you help me set up QuickBooks?

Yes — including building a chart of accounts that actually matches how your business operates, and training you so you're not guessing at the options.

How do I properly close or dissolve my business?

There are specific filings and procedures at the federal, state and county level. Skipping them is how business owners end up with notices and penalties years after they thought they were done. I can walk you through the full sequence.
