Deadline FAQ

Quick answers

When is my personal income tax return due?

April 15. Estimated income tax payments are due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15.

When are corporate tax returns due?

March 15 for both C corporations and S corporations. Corporate estimated payments are due March 15, June 15, September 15 and December 15.

When are New York sales tax returns due?

Annual filers: March 20. Quarterly filers: March 20, June 20, September 20 and December 20. Monthly filers: the 20th of each month.

When do W-2s and 1099s have to go out?

Recipient copies are due January 31 and IRS copies are due February 28. Annual W-2 and 1099 filing is one of the services I handle.

When is the non-profit Form 990 due?

May 15.
