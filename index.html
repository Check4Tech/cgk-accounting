Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What does CGK Accounting Services do?

CGK Accounting Services provides tax preparation and planning, bookkeeping, accounting and financial statements, and small business consulting. Clients include individuals, self-employed people, corporations, partnerships, non-profits, and fire districts and EMS agencies — primarily on Long Island, New York.

Who will I actually be working with?

Chris Kamine, the owner. CGK Accounting Services is a one-person firm, so the person you speak with on the phone is the person preparing your return and reconciling your books. Nothing is handed off to junior staff.

Do you charge for consultations?

No. Advice and consultations are free and considered part of the service. Questions and answers are never billed separately.

Is CGK Accounting Services registered to prepare tax returns?

Yes. CGK Accounting Services is registered as a tax return preparer with both New York State and the IRS, and is certified by the IRS as an authorized e-file provider. The firm carries general liability and professional liability (malpractice) insurance and is an accredited business with an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau.

Do you work with fire districts and EMS agencies?

Yes. Alongside small business and individual clients, CGK Accounting Services has direct experience working with fire districts and EMS agencies. These organizations operate under tighter recordkeeping and reporting expectations than a typical small business, and fifteen years in public accounting — the last of them as an audit manager — apply directly to that work.

How quickly will I get a response?

Calls and emails are returned the same business day — guaranteed. Messages sent through the website contact form receive a reply within 24 to 48 hours.

How much does this cost?

Substantially less than the larger accounting firms. Fees depend on the scope of the work, and you get a quote up front at no charge. Call (631) 908-5130 to talk through your situation.
