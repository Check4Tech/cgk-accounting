Four engagement levels

Pick the cadence that fits your business and your budget

CGK Accounting Services can act as your accounting department, your controller, or even your CFO. Several options are available to suit your needs.

Weekly onsite

The most hands-on option. Regular visits to your office to pay invoices, process payroll, track accounts receivable, maintain your checkbook balance, enter data and reconcile your financial records. Best for businesses that need daily financial help and want maximum freedom to focus on operations.

Best for: higher transaction volume, active payroll, offices that need a regular on-site presence.

Monthly

A perfect solution for businesses that write their own checks and make their own deposits. If you cannot always keep an accurate daily checkbook balance — or there is not always plenty of extra cash in the bank — you risk overdrawing, unnecessary bank fees and returned checks. Your checkbook and cash accounts get reconciled and corrected every month.

Best for: owners who want peace of mind that cash is accounted for and the checkbook is accurate.

Quarterly — most popular

Quarterly bookkeeping is the most popular option and satisfies the needs of most businesses with quarterly regulatory filings. Ideal if you need quarterly payroll tax returns prepared or sales tax returns filed, and it keeps your financial records current for tax planning.

Best for: businesses with quarterly payroll tax or sales tax obligations.

Annual

The most affordable option, available to smaller businesses with a limited number of transactions during the year. If you do not write many checks and make only a few deposits, this produces a complete set of financial records so your annual business tax return can be prepared properly.

Best for: low-volume businesses and side businesses.

Always included

You always get a complete set of books

As part of every engagement, a complete set of books is provided to you for your records — electronically or in hardcopy, whichever you prefer. Your records belong to you.

Bookkeeping FAQ

Common questions

How often should my books be done?

It comes down to transaction volume. Weekly onsite suits businesses that need daily help with invoices, payroll and receivables. Monthly suits owners who write their own checks but cannot keep an accurate running balance. Quarterly is the most popular and fits most businesses with quarterly payroll or sales tax filings. Annual is the most affordable option for smaller businesses with limited transactions.

Do I get a copy of my books?

Yes — always. A complete set of books is provided to you for your records as part of the engagement, either electronically or in hardcopy format.

Can you act as my accounting department?

Yes. Depending on what you need, CGK Accounting Services can function as your accounting department, your controller, or even your CFO.

Which option is the most affordable?

Annual bookkeeping. It is designed for smaller businesses with a limited number of transactions and produces everything needed to file the annual business return.
